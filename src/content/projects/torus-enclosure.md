Medical monitoring wearables face a compound design challenge: they must be clinically reliable, comfortable enough for extended wear, robust against real-world conditions, and manufacturable without specialist tooling.

### Ergonomic Form Development
The physical enclosure was contoured to distribute contact pressure evenly across varied wrist and forearm physiologies, minimizing skin irritation over prolonged monitoring cycles.

### Material Selection & Sealing
Material testing focused on biocompatible resins and elastomers capable of maintaining ingress protection against sweat and moisture while remaining feasible for rapid iterative 3D printing and tooling.
