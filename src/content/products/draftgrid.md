## The Idea

Most writing tools assume you already know what you want to say. DraftGrid is built for the messier reality: you have a complex technical idea, many connected parts, and you need to find the structure before you can write the document.

It is a structured drafting canvas - not a document editor, not a notes app, but a space for building architecture before prose.

## Who It Is For

Engineers writing technical reports, researchers drafting papers, and product builders who need to think before they type. People who find blank documents paralyzing because they need structure first.
