## The Idea

Ideas rarely arrive in isolation. They connect to other ideas, reference earlier work, contradict assumptions and invite questions. AraLoom is built to map those connections spatially - creating a visual web you can navigate and refine.

It is not a mind map. It is a knowledge environment - a place to see how things relate, not just list what they are.
